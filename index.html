Three habits that make chord changes easier

The Pivot Finger

When you switch from Am to C, keep your index and middle fingers glued to the fretboard. Only your ring finger moves, from the 2nd fret of the G string up to the 3rd fret of the A string.

The Hover Rule

Keep your fingers curved and hovering just above the frets between chords. The shorter the trip back to the strings, the faster and cleaner the change.

The 2-Minute Silent Switch

Set a timer and swap between two chord shapes without strumming at all. Go slowly, check that every string rings, then repeat. This is how muscle memory gets built.
